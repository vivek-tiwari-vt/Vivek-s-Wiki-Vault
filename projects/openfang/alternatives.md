# OpenFang comparisons and alternatives

## Scope of comparison

OpenFang documentation compares against frameworks commonly used for multi-agent automation.

## Positioning notes

- Differentiator claim: Rust native, single-binary runtime, and built-in autonomous hands.
- Alternative patterns in ecosystem often emphasize developer composition (orchestrators) over OS-like packaging.

## Validation checklist before selection

- Confirm required channel coverage.
- Confirm provider support/fallback behavior for your model stack.
- Validate security posture in operational tests.
- Validate deployment model (binary, docker, desktop, API).
